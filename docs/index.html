The rumor network

The network is a hand-built map of how a rate-cut rumor travels: 2 rumor sources (the FOMC, a Fed speaker), 3 media channels (wires, social, TV), 7 market participants (algos, desks, market makers, bond desks, economists, retail, options), and 8 market outcomes (fed funds futures, 2Y and 10Y yields, S&P 500, Nasdaq, VIX, the dollar, gold). Edges are information-flow links, weighted by how fast the channel carries news.

The rings let you see where the rumor is: gold rings start it, teal rings transmit it, purple rings trade on it, green rings are the prices that move. A green node lighting up means the market moved — the chips below the graph read out each move as it happens.
