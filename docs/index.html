The real event behind it

On September 11, 2026, August CPI printed 3.4% — with core at 0.3% against 0.2% expected. Hike odds jumped to 85%. Five days later the Fed raised 25bp to 3.75–4.00%, its first hike since 2023, and the dot plot pointed to one more this year. The 10Y closed at 5.006%, the S&P fell 0.45%, the dollar rose 0.7%, and gold tumbled to $4,310.

The network is a hand-built map of how that repricing traveled: 2 rumor sources (the FOMC, Chair Warsh), 3 media channels (wires, social, TV), 7 market participants (algos, desks, market makers, bond desks, economists, retail, options), and 8 market outcomes (hike odds, 2Y and 10Y yields, S&P 500, Nasdaq, VIX, the dollar, gold). Edges are information-flow links, weighted by how fast the channel carries news.

The rings let you see where the repricing is: gold rings start it, teal rings transmit it, purple rings trade on it, green rings are the prices that move. A green node lighting up means the market moved — the chips below the graph read out each move as it happens.
