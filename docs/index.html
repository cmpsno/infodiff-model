Independent Cascade

The dataset

Wayne Zachary studied a university karate club for three years, mapping friendships between its 34 members. Partway through, a conflict between the instructor ("Mr. Hi") and the club president ("Officer") split the group in two — and Zachary showed the split was largely predictable from the friendship network alone. It has since become one of the most-used toy networks in graph theory.

Here, that ground-truth split is repurposed as a lens on the cascade: the faction rings let you see whether a rumor stays inside one social circle or jumps the fracture line.
