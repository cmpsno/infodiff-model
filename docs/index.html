The real event behind it

The timeline above is the ground truth: CPI at 3.4% on September 11, hike odds to 85%, the 10Y topping 5% before the Fed acted, then 25bp to 3.75–4.00% on September 16 — the first hike since 2023. The network is the model's version of the same five days: 2 rumor sources (the FOMC, Chair Warsh), 3 media channels (wires, social, TV), 7 market participants (algos, desks, market makers, bond desks, economists, retail, options), and 8 market outcomes (hike odds, 2Y and 10Y yields, S&P 500, Nasdaq, VIX, the dollar, gold). Edges are information-flow links, weighted by how fast the channel carries news.

The rings let you see where the repricing is: gold rings start it, teal rings transmit it, purple rings trade on it, green rings are the prices that move. A green node lighting up means the market moved — the chips below the graph read out each move as it happens, marked against what really happened.
