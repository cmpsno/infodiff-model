What the model can't see

1. The supply channel

Borrowers compete for bonds whether or not anyone whispers. AI data-center debt, federal deficits, and war-driven oil prices moved the 10Y with no rumor needed. The network has no node for supply.

2. The political channel

A president demanding 1% on Truth Social is its own broadcast layer — reaching voters, not traders. The model treats all chatter as market chatter.

3. The priors channel

Jackson Hole in August did half the work. Diffusion models start at the event; real markets start at the expectation.

"The model gets the plumbing right — who hears it first, and how prices follow — but it misses the politics and the supply side that actually moved the bond market."
